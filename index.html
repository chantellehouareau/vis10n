The perspective

RayJ Dennis brings a rare combination of elite athletic performance, strategic business perspective, and entrepreneurial vision to his role as Founder and CEO of VIS10N.

His path from varsity basketball in Illinois, through Division I competition and into the NBA, has given him a direct understanding of the systems, opportunities, and pressures that define the modern sports landscape.

That experience informs how he builds. Not from the outside, but from within.

The path

From the court to the capital stack.

  1. PLAINFIELD & OSWEGO, IL

    Raised in Illinois

    Born in Plainfield and raised between Plainfield and Oswego, he came through the varsity program at Oswego East High School and was named third-team all-state as a junior.

  2. 2019 – 2021

    Boise State

    Started his Division I career in the Mountain West, leading the Broncos in assists and steals as a sophomore.

  3. 2021 – 2023

    Toledo

    Unanimous MAC Player of the Year and First-Team All-MAC in 2023, leading the Rockets to a 27–8 record and the MAC regular-season title.

  4. 2023 – 2024

    Baylor

    All-Big 12 Second Team. His 236 assists rank third for a single season in Baylor program history.

  5. 2025

    The NBA, and VIS10N

    Made his NBA debut with the Indiana Pacers in January 2025, and founded VIS10N the same year to operate within a changing sports landscape.

  6. 2025 – PRESENT

    Atlanta Hawks

    Signed with the Atlanta Hawks on a two-way contract. Alongside his career, he leads VIS10N and co-founded Luxe Assist AI within the VIS10N ecosystem.

Who we are

VIS10N Holdings (“VIS10N”, “we”, “us”) is a global holding company operating across sport, media, marketing, technology and real estate. We are responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, and a small amount of technical information needed to run the site.

  • Enquiries. When you use the “Work with us” form we collect your name, email address, organisation, stage, enquiry type, sectors and your message.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Information stored on your device. Your browser keeps a note that you have seen the opening animation, and a draft of an unsent enquiry so you do not lose it. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To read and respond to your enquiry and assess a potential partnership.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or third-party advertising.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. The only browser storage it uses is described above, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the site. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Netlify hosts the website and receives form submissions.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the site’s typefaces and a scrolling library, and may see your IP address when they do.
  • Our email provider receives the enquiry notifications we send to our team.

Some of these providers may store data outside your country. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and legal safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting relationship, and generally no longer than three years after our last contact, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. To make a request, contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy
